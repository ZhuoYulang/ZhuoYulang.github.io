I am a Lecturer at the College of Computer Science, Inner Mongolia University (内蒙古大学). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Inner Mongolia University in June 2026. My research interests include multimodal emotion recognition, multimodal sentiment analysis, robust learning with missing modalities, and emotion-intent joint understanding in multimodal conversation.

I have published papers in venues such as ACM MM, IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing, ICASSP, INTERSPEECH, and Data Intelligence. If you are seeking academic cooperation in multimodal affective computing, please feel free to email me at zuohaolin_0613@163.com.
